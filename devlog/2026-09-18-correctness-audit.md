## Claims that remain unverified or need a narrower frame

These are not all proven false. They should not be described as fact-checked yet.

1. **02.03:** Verify the exact Hawking quotation and its date. Distinguish Musk’s Mars objective from Thiel’s investment in space ventures; investment does not establish a personal Mars-settlement program.
2. **04.02:** Malthus’s argument is compressed into a universal resource-exhaustion-and-crash model. Use his full name, Thomas Robert Malthus, and distinguish the essay from later ecological models. “Robert Malthus” is not a different person.
3. **04.03:** Date and source “79 million tons CO2 per day”; distinguish fossil/industrial emissions from all emissions, and metric tonnes from US tons. Acidification is a direct effect of CO2 uptake, not simply a knock-on effect of warming. The existing pH direction correction is correct.
4. **04.04:** Claims that colony collapse alone could end our way of life need a defined mechanism and distinction between managed honeybees and other pollinators. The Midway plastic tonnages need a specific source and year. The garbage patch is not a solid island; the existing suspended-particles description is directionally helpful.
5. **04.05:** Ausubel’s claims about land sparing are turned into “more green cover … than historically.” Specify region, period and whether the measure is forest area, vegetation cover, or agricultural land. Retain attribution. [The cited talk](https://longnow.org/seminars/02015/jan/13/nature-rebounding-land-and-ocean-sparing-through-concentrating-human-activities/).
6. **05.04:** Source the agricultural-land share and “most rivers”; replace “may be altering” temperature with wording consistent with the essay’s earlier attribution. Extinction percentages need taxonomic scope (especially marine species versus all life), and the K–Pg percentage changes within this same passage. The 30-million-year recovery claim needs a defined ecosystem and study.
7. **06.03:** Identify the June 2015 decarbonization declaration and distinguish a political statement from a treaty awaiting ratification. The Haiti response claim is too sweeping without a particular intervention and evidence.
8. **08.02:** Identify the fish-bank project’s actual location and participants from the video before asserting it is in Bali. The Bali illustration alone cannot establish the project location.
9. **08.04:** The place-name Gopalpura/Alwar appears internally coherent. Verify the quoted passage and village count against the cited EcoTippingPoints source before treating the exact quantities as confirmed.
10. **08.05:** Distinguish gross Deepwater Horizon discharge estimates from net oil entering the Gulf and later legal estimates. The claim that BP possessed particular models and that they were not public is explicitly speculative; frame it as a question or documented access problem.
11. **08.07:** Source Lake Merritt’s historical sewage/connection story and the bacterial-level superlative. “Closed” can misdescribe culverts and restricted tidal flow as total isolation. The heading spelling is corrected.
12. **08.09:** “Drought is a misnomer” is misleading: drought is a meaningful hydrological and meteorological concept. “One year supply left” needs its reservoir-storage context and must not imply all California water would disappear after a year. Check rankings, snowpack snapshots, groundwater ages, almond water footprints, and the $1.3 billion subsidence figure individually.
13. **08.10:** Source the exact fish-species count, 5,000-job estimate, water-supply share and delta-wide elevation generalization. Do not transfer longfin listing language onto delta smelt.
14. **Images:** The Indian fishing-net image illustrates the Oregon Nestlé passage; it is not evidence of that location. Other illustrative photos similarly should not acquire literal place-specific alt text without verification. No image substitutions were made.
15. **Dating:** June 24, 2015 is inferred from reference-entry timestamps, not a verified publication date. Keep that caveat in editorial records and avoid asserting `datePublished` in structured data. Domain registration does not prove essay publication.
