# Writing with Anselm — Simulate World

The essay moves through feeling. An accurate edit fails if it destroys the rhythm, emotional register, or reason a passage is here. Read the surrounding chapter before changing a sentence. Make the smallest change that preserves both truth and voice.

## The movement to preserve

- Chapter 1: breathe, look at home, remember, feel attachment. Allow quiet and wonder. Do not bring the weight of a technical explanation into the opening.
- Chapter 2: dissonant public messages, existential dread, grief and anxiety. Famous voices amplify concern; their presence is not an endorsement. No relief yet.
- Chapter 3: emerging capabilities, growing understanding, collective successes, the possibility of care on a planetary scale. Optimism should feel earned and tentative, not triumphant.
- Chapter 4: complexity, agency, and the search for tools. We can shape outcomes; understanding connections helps us choose with care.
- Preserve the remaining chapters and their progression; this pass does not authorize a wholesale rewrite.

## Editing practice

Keep lyrical titles. Remove a first sentence when it merely repeats the title; do not remove deliberate echoes elsewhere by formula. Preserve specific words that carry feeling, such as “vivid.”

Put dates, image provenance, scientific background, and necessary supporting detail in brief optional image captions or linked notes when they interrupt the essay. Captions belong beneath the image, centered, italic, smaller, and narrower than the image. Essay headings remain distinct and return to the body’s reading line. Do not give every image a caption by default.

Accuracy should constrain the claim, not dictate an academic tone. Prefer a short truthful phrase to a paragraph of correction: “brought polio close to eradication.” Never restore an inaccurate claim for musicality. Keep mechanisms and qualifications in the research record unless the reader needs them to understand the argument.

Treat the essay’s present as circa 2015. Do not import later deaths, reputations, or outcomes into its narration. Nature has value in itself. Human agency and useful technology should serve the living world; the Kardashev scale is an imaginative prompt, not a mandate for domination or unlimited consumption.

Hyperlink useful names and concepts unobtrusively. A source for a film is not proof of an image’s artist or reuse license. Record uncertain attribution honestly in the devlog; do not invent credits.
